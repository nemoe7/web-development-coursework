Flower Ball 2002
With 12 rounded petals and smiling faces, Takashi Murakami's flower balls are celebrated for their display of joy and innocence. The Japanese artist's fascination with flowers began when he worked as a schoolteacher in the 1980s. Every other day, the artist would buy fresh blooms for his students to practice drawing.
